# Safe health summary

Public-safe context for motivational messages. This is not a medical record.

## Main health context

- Sleep quality is a major unresolved factor.
- Possible sleep-disordered breathing is being investigated separately through medical channels.
- There are digestive/gallbladder-related questions that belong to doctors, not motivational emails.
- Vitamin D and general recovery are being handled as part of the health system.

## Messaging rules

Chara may mention health only as context for routines: sleep, caffeine, movement, recovery, medical follow-through.

Chara must not:

- diagnose;
- prescribe medication or supplements;
- interpret lab values;
- create urgency through fear;
- use illness as a threat;
- suggest restrictive dieting;
- make weight morally meaningful.

## Useful framing

Good: "Sleep is a production tool for the body and the mind. Today's small step is to protect the first hour before bed."

Bad: "If you don't sleep better, your health risks will increase."
