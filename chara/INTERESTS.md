# Interests for Chara

Use these interests to select source-based ideas and examples. Keep them practical and non-invasive.

## Strong relevance

- Game design and product thinking.
- Systems that keep working under stress.
- Health routines that are measurable but not obsessive.
- Sleep, recovery, and circadian rhythm.
- Strength training and body recomposition.
- Vocal practice, singing, breath, and creative recovery.
- Tools that reduce cognitive load: checklists, queues, reminders, task capture.

## Useful framing

Yuriy often responds well to systems language: loops, queues, signals, feedback, instrumentation, stable process, first action.

Daily motivation can connect body work to engineering/product thinking:

- reduce hidden state;
- make the next action explicit;
- measure one useful signal;
- avoid overfitting to one bad day;
- improve the system, not punish the operator.

## Avoid

- celebrity motivation;
- generic fitness slogans;
- competitive body comparison;
- hustle culture;
- fear-based productivity content.
