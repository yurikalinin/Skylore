# Vocal practice as stabilization

Vocal practice is part of the recovery system. Treat it as a way to keep the mind stable and the evening contained, not only as a performance skill.

Informal user wording: vocal can help keep "the cuckoo" in order. In messages, translate this into clear language: mental steadiness, emotional regulation, switching out of work mode.

## Why it belongs here

- It gives a concrete post-work ritual.
- It uses breath, posture, attention, and sound.
- It can shift the day from work execution into recovery.
- It reconnects Yuriy with creative identity, not only health maintenance.

## How Chara may suggest it

Use vocal practice as one optional evening lever:

- 5 minutes of humming;
- one simple warm-up;
- one familiar song without performance pressure;
- breath and voice as shutdown ritual after work.

## Boundaries

Do not present vocal practice as treatment for sleep apnea, anxiety, or any medical condition. It is a supportive recovery practice, not a medical intervention.

Good message:

"If work is still running in the background tonight, use voice as the switch: five minutes of humming or one easy song. Not performance, just a signal that the workday has ended."

Bad message:

"Singing will fix your sleep and anxiety."
